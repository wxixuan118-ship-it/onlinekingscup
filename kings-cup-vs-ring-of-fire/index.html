Quick answer: King's Cup and Ring of Fire usually refer to the same draw-a-card party game. The setup and core loop are alike, while individual card meanings and the center-cup finale vary by region and group.

What They Have in Common

  • A standard deck arranged face down around a center cup.
  • Players draw clockwise and each rank triggers a rule.
  • Rhyme, Categories, Question Master, and Waterfall commonly appear.
  • Kings usually control the center-cup finale.

Common Differences

“King's Cup” is common in North America, while “Ring of Fire” is widely used in the UK and other regions. Ring of Fire groups may emphasize not breaking the card circle. Rules for 5, 6, Jack, and Ace differ most often.
