About Kings Cup Online

Kings Cup Online is an independent, free browser-based card game and rules resource. It was created to make Kings Cup—also known as Ring of Fire or Circle of Death—easy to play without downloading an app or creating an account.

What we publish

We maintain the interactive card game, card-by-card reference guides, setup instructions, variations, and non-alcoholic alternatives. The site content and game logic are maintained directly by the Kings Cup Online team rather than imported from an affiliate feed or content aggregator.

Responsible play

Participation is always optional. Never pressure anyone to drink. Use water or another non-alcoholic beverage, set personal limits, never drive after drinking, and stop immediately if anyone feels unwell. The Family mode is designed for non-alcoholic play.
