App Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This policy covers the Online Kings Cup Android application (package com.onlinekingscup.app) distributed on Google Play. It does not cover the onlinekingscup.com website, which is governed by our separate website privacy policy.

Short version: the app collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs and no network requests. Every card, rule and screen is bundled inside the app and runs offline.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device information. We have no servers that receive data from the app, and no analytics or crash-reporting service is embedded in it.

Data stored on your device

To make the app usable between sessions, it saves a small amount of information in local app storage on your device only:

  • the player names you type on the setup screen;
  • the game mode you last selected;
  • your sound on/off setting and chosen challenge-timer length;
  • a flag recording that you confirmed you are 18 or older.

This information never leaves your device. It is not readable by us or by anyone else, and it is deleted permanently when you uninstall the app or clear its storage from Android Settings.

Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, files, location or identifiers.

Links to our website

The About screen contains links to pages on onlinekingscup.com. Opening one of those links leaves the app and hands the address to your device's browser, at which point our website privacy policy applies. The app itself makes no request to the site.

Children

Online Kings Cup is a party game containing references to alcohol and is intended solely for adults aged 18 or older, or the legal drinking age where you live. The app is not directed at children, is rated for adults on Google Play, and shows an age confirmation screen on first launch. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects data, this policy and the app's Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before that version is published. The date above shows the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us via our About page.
