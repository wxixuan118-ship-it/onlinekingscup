How to Play King's Cup

Learn how to play King's Cup step by step: what you need, setup, turn order, card rules, the fourth King, and non-drinking options.

Play King's Cup Online

Quick answer: To play King's Cup, arrange a shuffled deck around a center cup, draw one card per turn, and follow the rule assigned to that card. The classic round ends when the fourth King is drawn.

What You Need

  • A standard 52-card deck; Jokers are optional.
  • Three to ten players, with four to eight ideal.
  • A center cup for the traditional version, or a challenge bowl for a non-drinking version.
  • A visible King's Cup card meanings reference.

How to Play Step by Step

  1. Shuffle the deck and place the cards face down in a ring around the center cup.
  2. Agree on every card meaning, house rules, opt-outs, and how the game will end.
  3. Choose a starting player and continue clockwise.
  4. On each turn, draw one card and complete its action before moving on.
  5. Track the Kings. The first three King drawers add a small contribution; the fourth completes the agreed finale.
  6. End on the fourth King or finish the deck—choose before play.

A Sample First Round

If the first player draws a 4, everyone safely touches the floor and the last player takes the agreed action. If the next player draws a Queen, that player becomes Question Master until another Queen appears. Persistent roles remain active while later turns continue.

How to Play Online

Open the game on one device and pass it around, or share the screen during a video call. The virtual deck handles shuffling and displays each instruction. One host may click for everyone, but keep a fixed turn order.
