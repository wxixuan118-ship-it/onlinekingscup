What Makes a Good Replacement?

A good alternative is short, observable, accessible, and easy to explain in one sentence. Avoid rules based on private disclosures, identity assumptions, dangerous movement, or pressure to consume anything.

Build a Custom Deck

Choose no more than three replacements for the first game. If you want a completely new set, use the King's Cup rule generator, then read the final meanings aloud before shuffling.
