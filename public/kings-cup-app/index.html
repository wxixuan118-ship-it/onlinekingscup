Kings Cup App for Android

The same Kings Cup you play here, installed on your phone. Shuffle, tap, read the rule out loud and pass the phone — even with no signal.

On iPhone or a laptop? Play in your browser — no install needed.

Quick answer: Online Kings Cup is free on Google Play for Android. It has 5 game modes, a full 54-card deck, 2–10 players and works completely offline, with no account and no ads.

Built for a Real Party

  • A full 54-card deck, freshly shuffled every round
  • 2 to 10 players, with your own names and automatic turn order
  • King counter — watch the cup fill and see who draws the fourth King
  • Built-in challenge timer: 10, 30 or 60 seconds
  • Card-rule reference for all five modes, readable without leaving the game
  • The screen stays awake while you play; sound and haptics can be switched off

Works Completely Offline

Every card, rule and screen is bundled inside the app. It works on a plane, in a basement or on a camping trip — anywhere with no signal. There is nothing else to download after install.

No Account, No Ads, No Tracking

The app collects no data: no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no network requests. Read the full app privacy policy.
