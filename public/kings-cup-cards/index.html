Why Card Meanings Differ

King's Cup is passed from group to group, so 5, 6, Jack, and Ace often have regional meanings. There is no single mandatory deck. Choose one set before the first draw and avoid changing a rule only after somebody receives the card.

How to Remember the Cards

Rhymes help with Two You, Three Me, Four Floor, Seven Heaven, Eight Mate, and Nine Rhyme. Keep this page open for the face cards and persistent roles. Each card above links to a short tool page with its common variation.
