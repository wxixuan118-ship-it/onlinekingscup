Quick answer: King's Cup house rules are group-specific additions or replacements. Agree on them before the first card, keep them visible, and give every player a no-explanation-needed pass.

Six House Rules Worth Setting

  1. Answer timer: allow three to five seconds in Rhyme and Categories.
  2. No repeats: an answer already used in a round cannot be used again.
  3. Pass rule: every player gets at least one unconditional pass.
  4. Rule cap: keep no more than three custom persistent rules active.
  5. Circle rule: decide whether breaking the card ring has a consequence.
  6. Ending: stop at the fourth King or finish the deck.

Fair-Play Rules

Do not create rules aimed at one person's identity, ability, private life, or consumption. Safety questions and genuine rule clarification should not trigger Question Master. A player may replace any prompt without explaining why.

Fun Custom Rule Ideas

  • Speak in a chosen accent until the next Jack.
  • No first names until the next Queen.
  • Before drawing, compliment another player.
  • Use only your non-dominant hand.
  • Add one word to a continuing group story.

Keep the Rules Visible

Write persistent rules on paper or in a shared note. For a custom starting point, use the rule generator and edit any result that does not fit your group.
