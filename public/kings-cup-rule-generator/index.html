Build a Balanced Custom Deck

Keep reaction rules, word games, one-turn prompts, and persistent roles in balance. Avoid making every card a long discussion. For a first custom game, replace only three entries from the standard King's Cup cards.

Rule Quality Check

A usable rule takes less than a minute, has a clear ending, works for every player, and does not require alcohol, dangerous movement, humiliation, or private disclosure.
