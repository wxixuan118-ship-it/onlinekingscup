How to Play King's Cup Without Alcohol

Play King's Cup without alcohol using points, trivia, movement, mocktails, and a safe replacement for the fourth King.

Play King's Cup Online

Quick answer: King's Cup does not require alcohol. Replace every drink instruction with points, a sip of any non-alcoholic beverage, trivia, or a short challenge, and replace the center cup with a challenge bowl.

The game runs on its card prompts — reaction challenges, word games, temporary roles, and the four-King build-up. None of that depends on what is in anyone's glass. Pick one substitution system below, use it for every drink instruction, and the pace and suspense stay exactly the same.

Four Easy Substitution Systems

Points

Each lost challenge adds one point; lowest score wins.

Trivia

Answer a quick question instead of drinking.

Movement

Use accessible actions such as claps, poses, or stretches.

Mocktails

Use small sips of water, soda, juice, or another chosen drink.

Replace the King's Cup

Put an empty bowl in the center. Each King drawer adds a written prompt. The fourth King drawer selects one prompt, or the group completes it together. Another option is to collect the letters K-I-N-G; the fourth drawer answers a final trivia question.

Adapt Persistent Rules

Mate can share points, Question Master can award a point for an accidental answer, and Thumb Master can give a harmless challenge to the slowest player. The card sequence stays unchanged.

Good Fits for a Sober Game

  • Dry January or a sober-curious month — keeps game night going without the drinks.
  • Workplace and team events — use the points or trivia system so nobody feels pressured.
  • Family nights with teens — movement and silly prompts work for a wide age range.
  • Designated drivers and anyone taking medication — a routine opt-out means no explaining.

Hosting Tips

Tell guests in advance that this is a non-alcoholic game, offer several substitutions, and make opting out routine. The game works because of quick social prompts — not because of what anyone drinks.
