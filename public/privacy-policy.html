Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This policy explains how Kings Cup Online processes information when you visit onlinekingscup.com. The game does not require an account and we do not ask you to submit your name, email address, payment information, or precise location.

Information processed automatically

Our hosting and security providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, requested URL, referring page, approximate region, timestamps, and diagnostic logs to deliver and protect the site.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage. Google may process device identifiers, IP-derived location, page views, referral information, and cookie or similar identifier data. Learn how Google uses information from partner sites at Google's partner-sites policy.

Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with the site through behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session recordings. Clarity may process interaction, device, browser, and diagnostic data using cookies or similar technologies. Sensitive form data is not intentionally collected by this site.

Typeform presale forms

We use Typeform to collect PartyDeck presale interest. When you choose to submit the form, Typeform processes the information you provide, such as your name, email address, country or region, game preferences, group information, and optional comments. Do not submit sensitive personal information. Typeform may also process technical and usage data needed to provide and secure the form.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We may use Google AdSense after the site is approved. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads. Google's advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to show ads based on visits to this site or other sites.

You can manage personalized Google advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also learn about industry opt-out choices at AboutAds.

Consent and choices

Where required, non-essential analytics or advertising storage will be controlled through an appropriate consent mechanism. Browser settings can also block or delete cookies, although doing so may affect some site features.

Children

The main drinking-game content is intended for adults aged 18 or older. The site does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Non-alcoholic game options do not require an account or personal information.

Data retention and sharing

Analytics, hosting, and advertising providers retain data according to their own settings and policies. We do not sell personal information directly. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to host, secure, analyze, or fund the site, or when legally required.

Changes

We may update this policy when services or legal requirements change. The date above shows the latest revision.
