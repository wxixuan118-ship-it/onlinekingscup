What Does 3 Mean in King's Cup?

Three means “me.” The drawer takes the agreed action, then play moves clockwise. This rule has no lasting effect and does not create a special role.

Common 3 House Rule

Use one point, one sip of any beverage, or a quick movement such as a clap.

House rules differ by group. Confirm the meaning before the first card is drawn, allow anyone to choose a non-alcoholic alternative, and never pressure a player to participate.

3 Rule FAQ

What is the 3 rule called in King's Cup?

The common 3 rule is called Me. House rules can vary, so agree on the meaning before play.
