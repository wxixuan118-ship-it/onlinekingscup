What Does 4 Mean in King's Cup?

Four means “floor.” As soon as the card is revealed, players safely touch the floor or the agreed surface. The slowest player takes the action.

Common 4 House Rule

For accessibility, choose the tabletop, raise four fingers, or tap a visible object instead.

House rules differ by group. Confirm the meaning before the first card is drawn, allow anyone to choose a non-alcoholic alternative, and never pressure a player to participate.

What is the 4 rule called in King's Cup?
