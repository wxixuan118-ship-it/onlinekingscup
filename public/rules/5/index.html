5 card rule · Thumb Master

What Is 5 in King's Cup?

Quick answer first, then the common variation and the next card.

Play King's Cup Online

Quick answer: The player who draws a 5 becomes Thumb Master. When they put a thumb on the table, the last player to copy them drinks.

What Does 5 Mean in King's Cup?

The Thumb Master may trigger the challenge at any suitable moment. The role normally lasts until another 5 is drawn, when the new drawer takes over.

Common 5 House Rule

Some groups use “Guys” for 5. Thumb Master is a more inclusive option and works with every group.

House rules differ by group. Confirm the meaning before the first card is drawn, allow anyone to choose a non-alcoholic alternative, and never pressure a player to participate.
