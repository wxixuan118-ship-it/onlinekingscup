What Does 7 Mean in King's Cup?

Seven means “heaven.” Reveal the card, point upward, and identify the last response. The rule ends as soon as that player completes the agreed action.

Common 7 House Rule

Players who cannot raise a hand can look upward, say “heaven,” or use another agreed signal.

House rules differ by group. Confirm the meaning before the first card is drawn, allow anyone to choose a non-alcoholic alternative, and never pressure a player to participate.
